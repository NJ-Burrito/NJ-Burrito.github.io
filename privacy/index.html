Privacy policy

NJ-Story Publisher · Last updated: 2026-10-25

Who runs this

NJ-Story Publisher is a private app run by one person, Nader Alzghoul. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. Contact: naderalzghoulbusiness@gmail.com.

What the app does

The app uploads Nader's own animated episodes of NJ-Story to his own accounts: the Instagram account @nj.story.ar, the Facebook Page NJ-Story and the YouTube channel NJ-Story. It also reads public comments on his own posts to decide what the next episode should be about.

What the app accesses

Instagram

Used to publish episodes, read comments and read post insights on @nj.story.ar.

  • instagram_business_basic
  • instagram_business_content_publish
  • instagram_business_manage_comments
  • instagram_business_manage_insights

Facebook Page

Used to publish episodes and read comments and engagement on the NJ-Story Page.

  • pages_manage_posts
  • pages_read_engagement
  • pages_read_user_content
  • pages_manage_engagement
  • pages_show_list
  • business_management

YouTube (Google API)

youtube.upload uploads episodes to the NJ-Story channel. youtube.readonly reads the channel and public comments on its videos.

The app does not read direct messages on any platform.

What is stored and where

  • Access tokens for the three accounts. They are stored locally on Nader's own PC and nowhere else.
  • For each public comment on his own posts: the comment text, the username and the timestamp. Nothing else about the commenter is stored. This is kept locally on his PC.
  • The episodes themselves, which are his own work.

The app has no server and no database run by anyone else.

How long it is kept

Comments are kept to count votes and collect ideas for new episodes. They are deleted after 90 days unless Nader decides to keep a specific one. Tokens are kept until the access is revoked or the app is no longer used, then deleted.

No sharing

The data is not sold, rented, shared or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

NJ-Story Publisher's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice: Google user data is used only to upload to and read from the NJ-Story channel as described above. It is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not read by anyone except Nader.

Your choices and deleting your data

If a comment of yours was stored and you want it removed, write to naderalzghoulbusiness@gmail.com with your username and the post. It will be deleted.

Nader can end the app's access at any time in the Google account permissions page, in Facebook Business Integrations and in Instagram app settings. After that the stored tokens are deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
